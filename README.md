# Coffee Recipe Suggester - Espresso Edition

A beautiful and interactive web application that helps espresso machine owners discover coffee recipes based on their available ingredients and syrups.

## Features

- **Ingredient Selection**: Choose from a variety of milk types, syrups, sweeteners, and extras
- **Smart Recipe Matching**: Automatically finds recipes you can make with your selected ingredients
- **35+ Coffee Recipes**: From classic espresso to trendy shaken espressos and frappés
- **Match Scoring**: See how well each recipe matches your available ingredients
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Beautiful UI**: Modern, coffee-themed design with smooth animations

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Select the ingredients and syrups you have available by clicking the checkboxes
3. Click "Find Coffee Recipes" to see what you can make
4. Browse through the matched recipes with detailed instructions
5. Use "Clear All" to start over with a new selection

## Recipe Categories

The app includes recipes for:

- **Classic Espresso Drinks**: Espresso, Ristretto, Lungo, Americano
- **Milk-Based Drinks**: Cappuccino, Latte, Flat White, Macchiato, Cortado
- **Flavored Lattes**: Vanilla, Caramel, Hazelnut, Cinnamon, Pumpkin Spice
- **Chocolate Drinks**: Mocha, White Mocha, Nutella Latte

- **Blended Drinks**: Chocolate Chip Frappé, Caramel Frappé
- **Specialty Drinks**: Affogato, Con Panna, Breve
